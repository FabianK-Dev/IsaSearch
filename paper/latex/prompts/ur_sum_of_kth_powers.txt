Theorem (Euler's Polynomial Formula for Summation):

The total when adding up the squares, cubes or any other power r raised to each number from 1 through n can be represented as a polynomial function of n. This formula provides an algebraic expression that encapsulates this summative relationship between powers and their corresponding integers within a given range.
